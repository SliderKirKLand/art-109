02 / About

Interactive work for curious audiences.

I am Nhat Minh Le, an undergraduate Digital Media Art student interested in interactive design, motion graphics, and immersive installations.

I work with TouchDesigner, Blender, Adobe Creative Suite, projection, sound, space, and audience participation to build experiences people can enter and explore.

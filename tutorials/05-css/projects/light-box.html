Bring Imagination
into the Lightbox

Lightbox is a collaboration project created by CADRE, Computers in Art, Design, Research, and Education, Student Organization where every team will group with 3 peers to build a lightbox project. In this project, students experience laser cutting with paper and wood to build the box. They share ideas and work on the art concept with Adobe Illustrator. Students also solder electrical boards to power the LED lights. In the final steps, each group assembles and glues the components, crafting the box before placing everything inside.

This project was showcased at Admitted Spartan Day 2024 , SubZERO2024 , and Maker Faire.

CADRE S.O. at SubZERO Festival 2024

Besides sharing these fun projects at San Jose State University, the CADRE Student Organization has created a public platform for students to showcase their creative works at the SubZERO Festival 2024 in Downtown San Jose. The students have built these projects together, finding joy in collaboration and building networks with their peers. This project created a memorable experience in Spring 2024 and brought people together in a spirit of creativity and camaraderie.
